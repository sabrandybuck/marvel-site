Week 6 · From counts to meaning

Who is close — and why?

Week 6 gave every page numbers that describe language — TF-IDF weights, topic mixtures, PPMI — and one recurring warning: a cosine without a close reading proves nothing. So we ask one question one character at a time: which pages look closest, are they actually linked, and what do the sentences say? Start on Storm and see for yourself.
